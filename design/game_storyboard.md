## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

An abandoned castle adventure where the player must explore dark and dangerous rooms, collect important items, and defeat the Shadow King.

**Storyline:**

The player becomes trapped inside an abandoned castle controlled by the Shadow King. To escape, the player must explore the castle and collect a Rusty Key, Flashlight, Health Potion, Silver Sword, Ancient Map, and Golden Amulet. Each item helps the player progress through the castle and prepare for the final encounter. After collecting all six items, the player must reach the Shadow King's chamber and use the Golden Amulet to defeat him and escape.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Castle Entrance: Start room
2. Storage Room: Rusty Key
3. Dark Hallway: Flashlight
4. Healing Chamber: Health Potion
5. Armory: Silver Sword
6. Ancient Library: Ancient Map
7. Treasure Room: Golden Amulet
8. Shadow King's Chamber: Villain room

Add more rooms if your design needs them.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Rusty Key — Unlocks the gate leading to the abandoned castle.
2. Flashlight — Illuminates dark areas and helps the player explore safely.
3. Health Potion — Restores the player's health after encountering enemies.
4. Silver Sword — Allows the player to defend against the villain's guards.
5. Ancient Map — Shows important locations and helps the player navigate the castle.
6. Golden Amulet — A powerful artifact needed to defeat the Shadow King and escape.

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The Shadow King — A powerful and mysterious villain who controls the abandoned castle. He has trapped the player inside and guards the final chamber. The player must collect all six items and use the Golden Amulet to defeat him and escape.
